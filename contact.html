For Prospective Students

We regularly supervise thesis projects at various levels (Bachelor's, Master's). If you're a student interested in evolutionary modelling, computational biology, or theoretical biology, we'd be happy to discuss potential projects.

Our projects typically involve developing simulation models in Python or C++, mathematical analysis of evolutionary dynamics, or a combination of both. No prior experience with evolutionary modelling is required, but enthusiasm for learning and curiosity about how evolution works are essential!
