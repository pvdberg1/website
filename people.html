Our Team

We are a diverse group of researchers united by our curiosity about how evolution shapes life. Meet the people behind the models.

Piet van den Berg

Principal Investigator

Piet leads the Evolutionary Modelling Group. His research focuses on microbial evolution (antibiotic resistance, evolvability, mutational robustness) as well as human cooperation and cultural evolution. The group works on a wide range of topics and collaborates closely with several empirical labs at KU Leuven and beyond.

Outside of work: Reading, cooking, music, movies, travelling, cycling, and sailing.

Fun fact: "Did you know I almost became a movie star at age 12? Studying evolutionary biology was my backup plan."

JL

Juliette Luiselli

Postdoctoral Researcher

Juliette's research focuses on the impact of chromosomal rearrangements on the evolution of coding and non-coding genome size. Ultimately, she aims to understand how genomes evolve to become robust to a wide range of mutations.

Outside of work: Cuddling cats, all types of sports (especially volleyball), and going on long bike trips!

"What fascinates me about evolution is that it led to people who question whether it can be true because something as perfect as the human eye exists, often while they are wearing glasses."

BlueSky
GB

Giorgio Boccarella

PhD Student

Giorgio's research focuses on the evolution of antibiotic resistance. He develops evolutionary lineage models to understand how bacterial populations evolve resistance, and how factors like persistence (a dormant survival state) impact the speed, predictability, and magnitude of resistance evolution.

AJ

Alger Jorritsma

PhD Student

Alger's research deals with the evolution of plasticity and evolvability. He aims to understand the interaction between them, both functionally and mechanistically, using gene regulatory network models to explore how organisms evolve the capacity to adapt to changing environments.

Outside of research: All kinds of sports (mostly bouldering, cycling, and running), reading, drawing, and planning cool bike trips.

"I often get inspired by people with crazy ideas."

Ronja Hulst

PhD Candidate

Ronja's research is about mutational robustness: how organisms evolve to tolerate mutations and what this means for adaptation and evolvability. She explores the fundamental trade-off between the need for stability and the capacity for evolutionary change.

Outside of research: All kinds of sports (especially kung fu!), travelling, hiking, going on adventures, music, dancing, gaming, philosophy, and reading.

"What fascinates me is how simple processes can give rise to life, complexity, and diversity. From the origin of life on Earth to the possibility of life elsewhere in the universe."

BlueSky

Kat Van der Poorten

PhD Student

Kat's research investigates the origins of language. She uses evolutionary models to understand how human language emerges, and what might have been the selective pressures that have led to the integration of pre-existing cognitive capacities.
